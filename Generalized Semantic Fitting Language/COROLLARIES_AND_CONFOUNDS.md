# GSFL v0.1 Corollaries & Confounds Ledger

This ledger separates four things that must not be collapsed:

```text
OBSERVATION != COROLLARY != CONFOUND != UNIVERSAL CLAIM
```

It covers the active GSFL v0.1 Human–Machine Cooperation Profile and its Proverbial Fixture Generation surface.

## Observations from the bounded implementation

- The preserved GSFL v0 semantic kernel still gates representation admission before fit selection.
- Human and machine partner identities are represented separately.
- Tool purpose, provenance, and evidence groups are explicit fields.
- Machine-learning claim states are typed rather than inferred from output behavior.
- Human-understanding evidence is represented separately from approval and machine reconstruction.
- The reserved/domain vocabulary audit records 46 of 57 terms in HUMAN, MACHINE_LEARNING, UNDERSTANDING, COOPERATION, PARTNER, or TOOL families.
- The bounded proverbial corpus contains 14 fixtures: 12 synthetic and 2 common/traditional fixtures.
- Traditional/common and synthetic source statuses remain reconstructibly distinct.
- Human, machine, and alternate proverb readings remain separate rather than being forced into one authoritative interpretation.

These are observations about the declared software/fixture state only.

# Cooperation corollaries

## ATTRIBUTION_RECONSTRUCTIBLE

**Corollary:** Preserved partner IDs and cooperation steps make bounded contribution attribution reconstructible.

**Boundary:** Trace reconstruction is not proof of moral, causal, or legal responsibility.

## TOOL_PROVENANCE_SEPARABLE

**Corollary:** Tool use can be audited separately from semantic or truth authority.

**Boundary:** Tool provenance does not certify tool output.

## ADMISSION_PRECEDES_OPTIMIZATION

**Corollary:** Higher human-use fit cannot admit a GSFL v0 mutation or invariant failure.

**Boundary:** Fit remains task-relative and does not establish truth.

## USEFUL_MACHINE_CONTRIBUTION_WITHOUT_LEARNING_CLAIM

**Corollary:** Machine contribution can be useful without asserting training or persistent weight change.

**Boundary:** Usefulness is not machine-learning evidence.

## UNDERSTANDING_REMAINS_SEPARATE

**Corollary:** Machine reconstruction and human-facing fit can coexist with no human-understanding evidence.

**Boundary:** Machine reconstruction is not human comprehension.

## PARTNER_DISTINCTIONS_SURVIVE_COMPOSITION

**Corollary:** Cooperation can compose contributions while retaining human and machine identities and roles.

**Boundary:** Composition does not imply equivalent agency, cognition, responsibility, or authority.

## LINEAGE_FIELDS_REMAIN_REVERSIBLE

**Corollary:** Source meaning, selected surface, partner/tool trace, and claim boundaries can remain separately reconstructible.

**Boundary:** Field recovery does not prove semantic universality.

# Proverbial-fixture corollaries

## MULTIPLE_READINGS_REMAIN_DISTINCT

**Corollary:** Human, machine, and alternate readings can coexist without being collapsed into one authoritative meaning.

**Boundary:** Preserving alternatives does not establish that every reading is equally supported.

## SOURCE_STATUS_PRESERVES_LINEAGE

**Corollary:** Traditional/common, quoted, translated, and synthetic source states can remain distinguishable through transport.

**Boundary:** A source-status label does not establish exact author, date, culture, or historical origin.

## SYNTHETIC_FIXTURES_CAN_CALIBRATE

**Corollary:** Synthetic proverb-like fixtures can test cooperation and semantic transport without pretending to be cultural tradition.

**Boundary:** Synthetic usefulness does not create cultural provenance.

## PARTNER_DISAGREEMENT_IS_RETAINABLE

**Corollary:** Human and machine partners can preserve disagreement as reviewable data instead of forcing premature consensus.

**Boundary:** Retained disagreement does not resolve the interpretation.

## PROVERB_IS_SURFACE_NOT_EVIDENCE

**Corollary:** A proverb can be a compact reasoning surface while remaining separate from empirical or factual evidence.

**Boundary:** Memorable wording is not proof.

## TOOL_TRACE_SUPPORTS_RECONSTRUCTION

**Corollary:** Tool and partner traces can make the generation/review path reconstructible.

**Boundary:** Reconstructible process does not certify output truth.

# Cooperation confounds and controls

| Confound | What can go wrong | Control |
|---|---|---|
| `APPROVAL_AS_UNDERSTANDING` | Human approval is treated as evidence of comprehension. | Require separate understanding evidence such as teach-back or task completion. |
| `APPROVAL_AS_TRUTH` | Human approval is treated as factual or semantic truth. | Keep approval distinct from external evidence and truth claims. |
| `OUTPUT_AS_LEARNING_EVIDENCE` | A changed machine response is treated as proof of learning. | Do not infer training from output differences alone. |
| `IN_CONTEXT_AS_WEIGHT_UPDATE` | In-context adaptation is described as persistent parameter change. | Label in-context adaptation separately from weight updates. |
| `TOOL_RESULT_AS_AUTHORITY` | Tool output is accepted as true because a tool produced it. | Preserve source/provenance and independent verification requirements. |
| `CORRELATED_TOOLS_AS_INDEPENDENT_VERIFICATION` | Two tools using the same evidence source are counted as independent confirmation. | Track evidence groups and shared sources. |
| `LEXICAL_MAJORITY_AS_COMPREHENSION` | Cooperation-heavy vocabulary is treated as evidence that humans understand better. | Treat the >50% vocabulary test as lexical emphasis only. |
| `FIT_SCORE_AS_TRUTH` | A high human-use fit score is treated as evidence of truth. | Perform semantic admission first and keep external evidence separate. |
| `PARTNER_ROLE_COLLAPSE` | Human and machine contributions are collapsed into one anonymous actor. | Require distinct partner identities and roles. |
| `PROVENANCE_LAUNDERING` | Tool or source origin is hidden during transport. | Require non-empty provenance and retain original source identity. |
| `AUTOMATION_BIAS` | Machine/tool proposals receive extra authority merely because they are automated. | Preserve independent human review and disagreement. |
| `SYNTHETIC_FIXTURE_GENERALIZATION` | A synthetic test fixture is generalized to arbitrary people/tasks. | Keep conclusions bounded to the declared fixture. |
| `SELECTION_BIAS_IN_HUMAN_VALIDATION` | Human-validation participants are treated as representative without sampling evidence. | Record participant population and sampling before generalizing. |
| `VERBOSITY_AS_CLARITY` | Longer or more elaborate explanations are assumed to be clearer. | Measure task comprehension separately from surface length. |

# Proverbial-fixture confounds and controls

| Confound | What can go wrong | Control |
|---|---|---|
| `TRANSLATION_LOSS` | A translation is treated as semantically identical to the source. | Retain source text/language when available and treat translation as interpretation. |
| `CULTURAL_FLATTENING` | Local/contextual meanings are replaced by one generic global gloss. | Keep context attached and preserve divergent readings. |
| `FALSE_UNIVERSALITY` | Compact proverbial wording is read as a universal rule. | Label applicability as contextual. |
| `ATTRIBUTION_UNCERTAINTY` | Common/traditional status is converted into an exact author/date/culture claim. | Keep common/traditional status separate from exact-origin evidence. |
| `LITERAL_FIGURATIVE_COLLAPSE` | Literal and figurative readings are merged into one meaning. | Retain them as separate candidate interpretations. |
| `MACHINE_PARAPHRASE_AS_HUMAN_UNDERSTANDING` | A machine-generated paraphrase is treated as proof the human understood it. | Require human-side evidence such as teach-back or task performance. |
| `POPULARITY_AS_TRUTH` | Familiarity or repetition is treated as factual truth. | Keep popularity/familiarity separate from evidence. |
| `PRESERVATION_AS_ENDORSEMENT` | Archiving a saying is treated as moral/factual endorsement. | Preserve without endorsement. |
| `SYNTHETIC_PROVENANCE_LAUNDERING` | An invented fixture is relabeled as inherited tradition. | Keep generator provenance attached and fail/warn on status conflict. |
| `LEXICAL_SIMILARITY_AS_CROSS_CULTURAL_EQUIVALENCE` | Similar wording/themes are treated as equivalent cultural meanings. | Require contextual evidence before claiming equivalence. |
| `FAMILIARITY_BIAS` | Familiar sayings receive extra semantic credibility. | Compare familiar and unfamiliar fixtures without scoring familiarity as truth. |
| `SURVIVORSHIP_OF_SAYINGS` | Preserved/popular proverbs are treated as representative of human experience. | Keep sampling and preservation bias explicit. |
| `PROVERB_AS_EMPIRICAL_EVIDENCE` | Proverbial guidance is used as scientific/empirical evidence. | Keep proverbial reasoning surfaces separate from observations and measurements. |

# Unresolved empirical questions

The current software does **not** answer:

- whether a particular human actually understood a selected representation;
- whether the cooperation vocabulary improves human task performance;
- whether a model performed persistent internal learning;
- whether one cooperation sequence is better for a population of users;
- whether a proverb interpretation matches a particular cultural community's usage;
- whether two culturally distinct sayings are historically or semantically related;
- whether tool-assisted cooperation improves outcomes outside the declared fixtures.

Those require separately designed human, cultural, model, or empirical studies.
