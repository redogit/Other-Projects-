# ATTACK Skill

## Purpose
Try to break the current distinction, grounding, transport, candidate repair, or interpretation with evidence that can actually discriminate among live alternatives.

## Preferred attacks
Use the smallest useful subset of:
- one-degree / one-factor contrasts;
- equivalent duplicates;
- negative controls;
- mutation controls with intervention activation proved;
- ablations and deletion attacks;
- carrier drops;
- alternate encodings;
- independent reconstruction/formalization/runtime where materially different;
- exhaustive finite checks when the universe is genuinely finite;
- justified out-of-distribution panels;
- congruence and representative-invariance attacks.

## Procedure
1. Freeze the attacked claim and claim ceiling.
2. Enumerate live alternatives/counterexamples.
3. Choose the cheapest test expected to separate them.
4. Prove the intervention actually changed the intended factor before interpreting its result.
5. Preserve PASS, FAIL, INVALID, negative, and unresolved outcomes separately.
6. Do not rewrite the attacked claim after seeing results; successors receive new identity.

## Output
`ATTACK_REPORT`: frozen target, attack type, intervention, activation check, observations, change, remainder, relation classification, verdict, counterexample/witness, scope, and next lawful pressure.

## Stop / handoff
- localized failure -> `REPAIR`
- attack reveals grounding defect -> `GROUND`
- attack reveals representation defect -> `TRANSPORT`
- no discriminator exists yet -> `DISTINGUISH`
- do not repair or promote from inside ATTACK.
