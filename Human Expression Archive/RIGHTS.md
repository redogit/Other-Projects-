# Rights and stewardship

This publication carries source-specific metadata and limited excerpts, not a blanket license over cultural expression. The original rights fields remain unchanged in every record. It grants no general model-training permission and assigns no Traditional Knowledge Label on any community's behalf.

Record a work, translation, performance, recording, community protocol and access permission separately. Keep attributed wording and editorial interpretation distinct. Do not infer that an entire people endorses a single witness, that a source's claim is true, or that a public catalogue licenses copying its media.

No complete lyrics, audio or video are included. Where permission or community authority is unresolved, retain that limitation and consult the named steward before further uses. A request to correct attribution, restrict access or amend a source should produce a versioned correction with recoverable provenance rather than an invented historical original.

Private Library files, source-head snapshots and personal-context material are outside this public package. The archive contains no consented human-learning study and must not be used to infer an individual's learning or cultural identity.
