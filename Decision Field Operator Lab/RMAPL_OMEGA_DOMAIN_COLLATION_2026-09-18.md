
Promotion requires:
1. at least one concrete native fixture;
2. a consequential role, not vocabulary similarity alone;
3. explicit native remainder;
4. round-trip or loss accounting;
5. no evidence/authority promotion;
6. a counterprobe showing when the abstraction should NOT apply.

Cross-domain repetition strengthens the case but does not itself prove universality.

## 9. Current collation conclusion

The conversation supports a common repair/fitting calculus, but the repository already demonstrates why the calculus must be domain-derived:

- S'1 contributes explicit trajectory/mirror/observer/replay mechanics.
- Decision Field contributes consequential distinctions, action selection, repair/branch control, and unresolved-state discipline.
- GSFL contributes semantic fit/reconstruction/decay separation.
- Hodge contributes the strictest evidence/authentication firewall.
- Image and dimensional fixtures contribute concrete projection/loss counterexamples.
- Suggest contributes generator-vs-authority separation.
- Conscience64 contributes research navigation and correction lineage without independent evidence weight.

The candidate RMAPL/Ω layer is therefore a **minimal shared inspectable carrier discovered from these native contracts**, not a master ontology over them.
