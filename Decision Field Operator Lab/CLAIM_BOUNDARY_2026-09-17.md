# Decision Field Claim Boundary — 2026-09-17

The parameterized Decision Field is currently:

- a software/research abstraction;
- a way to specify possibilities, consequential distinctions, relations, operators, evidence, goals and unresolved remainder;
- an exact interface for bounded operator experiments when domain functions are exact;
- a federation method for coordinating otherwise independent projects.

It is **not** currently established as:

- a universal law of nature;
- a universal theory of human decision-making;
- a proof of `P=NP` or `P!=NP`;
- a proof of the Hodge conjecture;
- a new interpretation or law of quantum mechanics;
- evidence of machine consciousness/personhood;
- a universal semantic parser or truth metric.

```text
SHARED_STRUCTURE != SHARED_MECHANISM
METHOD != DOMAIN_TRUTH
IMPLEMENTATION != UNIVERSALITY
```
